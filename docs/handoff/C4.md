# C4 Declension content
Status: done with gaps

## What was built
The stages `triggers`, `lessons` and `exercises` in `tools/pipeline/src/stages/`, the authored YAML in `content/authored/triggers/`, `patterns/` and `lessons/`, two model routes, and five validate checks.

| Piece | Where | Does |
|---|---|---|
| Triggers | `content/authored/triggers/*.yaml` | **221 triggers**: 60 prepositions (every primary one with each of its cases; na, v, o, po, za, medzi, nad, pod, pred as one row per case sharing a contrast group; compound and secondary prepositions), 119 verbs (acc, dat, gen, ins; *volať* acc/dat and *byť* nom/ins as contrast groups), 9 numerals (dva–štyri nom, päť and up gen), 7 quantity words, 6 container nouns, 6 adjectives (*plný*, *hodný* ...), 14 question words. Each row has an English meaning, an example and its translation. Every trigger id of the mini dataset is kept (C5's scenarios use them) |
| Morphology loader | `triggers/morphology.ts` | Reads `lemmas.jsonl` + `forms.jsonl` (the morph build plus freq ranks, or a content set with `--morph <dir>`), the forms of the lemmas a stage needs, and an index of every spelling with its best rank and case bits |
| `triggers` stage | `triggers/index.ts` | Checks ids and prefixes, contrast groups (2+ rows, one lemma, distinct cases), that each lemma exists, that each example's words are known and that the trigger is in it, and **every preposition's cases against `flags.cases` of the morphology in both directions**. In a complete build any problem throws; with `--morph` (a partial set such as the mini dataset) the missing lemmas are warnings, but a case mismatch still fails. Writes `content/build/triggers/triggers.jsonl` (part `triggers.jsonl`) and a report |
| Patterns | `content/authored/patterns/{nouns,adjectives}.yaml`, `lessons/patterns.ts` | 14 noun and 3 adjective patterns (*pekný*, *cudzí*, *otcov*). Each has its model word, an everyday word, the stem, the stem alternations (*žien*, *dlaň*), the preferred form where the model word has two (*chlapi*/*chlapovia*, *dievčatá*/*dievčence*) and an English note. **Endings are computed**: for each tag, the model word's real form minus the stem. A pattern whose model or everyday word follows another pattern in the morphology fails |
| Corpus weights | `lessons/weights.ts` | Sum of Araneum ARF per bucket over every common noun tag of the word-tag list (`content/raw/araneum-arf/`), vocative left out; without that file (tests, `--morph`) the top forms of ranked nouns. Each lesson's buckets get weights that sum to 1 (3 decimals) |
| Lessons | `content/authored/lessons/01-…09-*.yaml`, `lessons/index.ts` | Nine lessons: acc sg, loc sg, gen sg, ins sg, dat sg with *jemu*/*jej*, nom+acc pl, loc+ins+dat pl, gen pl (with numerals), mixed (every bucket). Each has five steps: explain (English markdown plus examples with a `[highlight]`, checked to be a form in the lesson's case), triggers, comprehension, production, checkpoint (pass share 0.75). The stage writes the steps with empty exercise lists; `exercises` fills them |
| Plan | `exercises/plan.ts` | For each bucket 50 slots (40 wanted × 1.25): a target noun (rank ≤ 3,000, of the bucket's gender), every 4th an adjective phrase (rank ≤ 1,500), every 3rd a pronoun in the dative lesson; the lesson's triggers of that case in turn; every 5th slot to the `mixed` lesson. The form of each word comes from the morphology, never from the model |
| Prompts | `exercises/prompts.ts` | `content.exercises` v1 (medium, 10 slots per request: bucket, triggers with a form to use and a meaning, vocabulary limit, names, items with the exact gap) and `content.exercises.review` v1 (medium, 25 per request: natural, translationCorrect, onlyThisCase, problem). Mocks: `<Trigger> <gap>.` (questions `<Koho>? <Gap>.`), the review passes all |
| Rules | `exercises/rules.ts` | `checkCandidate`: final punctuation, 2–14 words, no digits; the gap occurs exactly once; each gap word is a form of its lemma with the slot's tag; every other word is a known form with rank ≤ the vocabulary limit (8 names allowed); the trigger stands right before the gap (prepositions, numerals, quantity words, nouns, adjectives), in the sentence with no preposition before the gap (verbs), or before the gap (question words); a gap after a two-case trigger must not also be a form of the other case (*na dni*, *za brata*). Then the options, difficulty, kind and id |
| `exercises` stage | `exercises/index.ts` | Plan → open slots (those without a row in the generated file) → `content.exercises` → rules → `content.exercises.review` → rows. Rows go to `content/generated/exercises.jsonl` (live) or `content/build/exercises/exercises.mock.jsonl` (mock); the bank `content/build/exercises/exercises.jsonl` (part `exercises.jsonl`) and `lessons.jsonl` (a `merge` part that sets each lesson's `steps`: comprehension 12 choice items, production 12 and checkpoint 10 cloze items, round robin over the buckets, easiest first). A report lists every rejection with its reason |

Exercise rows: `id` = `ex-` + 10 hex digits of sha256(kind, sentence, span, answers, bucket, trigger). Kinds: every 4th slot a choice, `choice_meaning` (the contrast group's meanings, built by rule) on alternate turns for contrast triggers, else `choice_form` (the right form and 3 other case forms of the same number, an adjective agreeing; never the model's); the rest `cloze`. `difficulty` 0–1 from length, the rarest other word, the target's rank, phrase, plural and cloze. `source: generated`, `verified: false`, `flags: { slot, review: 'pass', model, generation {route, promptVersion, requestHash}, reviewer {…} }`.

Checks: `c4.trigger-cases` (each preposition trigger's case is in `flags.cases` and each case there has a trigger), `c4.patterns` (each ending ends a real form of the model word with that tag), `c4.lessons` (five steps in order with ords 0–4, no empty exercise step, checkpoint ≥ 8, each exercise of a step belongs to the lesson, weights sum to 1), `c4.exercises` (answers are forms of the target words in the row's case and number, and gender for adjectives; the gap is an answer; cloze has no options; one right option; `choice_form` options are real forms marked right exactly when they are answers; generated rows are reviewed and not verified; in a complete build a warning below 40 per bucket).

## How to run and test it
- `pnpm check` passes. The C4 tests (`triggers.test.ts`, `lessons.test.ts`, `exercises.test.ts`, 28 tests) take about 5 s.
- Full run, after `morph` and `freq`: `pnpm run pipeline triggers` (4 s) → `lessons` (30 s, reads the Araneum word-tag list) → `exercises --fill dry-run` → (approved) `exercises --fill live --max-spend 9` → `validate`. Options of `exercises`: `--per-bucket`, `--vocabulary`, `--buckets acc.sg.f,…`, `--exercises <file>`, `--morph <dir>`.
- Done when, and the tests:
  - Mini dataset: `triggers`, `lessons` and `exercises --fill mock` run with `--morph <mini>`; the mini set with its triggers, patterns, lessons (steps merged) and exercises replaced by the output passes `validate` with every check of every stage (the four C4 checks among them) and the mini registry.
  - Preposition cases: the authored triggers agree with `flags.cases` of the twelve mini prepositions; a `prep-s-gen` row fails `buildTriggers` and makes the `triggers` stage throw; in a set, `c4.trigger-cases` reports a trigger with the wrong case and the case left without a trigger.
  - Patterns: for every model word in the mini dataset (16 of 17; *otcov* is not there), stem + endings rebuild all 14 (noun) or 56 (adjective) forms, each a real form with that tag and the preferred one where there are two; a wrong stem fails; `c4.patterns` finds an ending of no real form.
  - Lessons: nine, each with its five steps in order; after the mock fill each checkpoint has 10 exercises (≥ 8) of its own lesson, comprehension only choice items.
  - Rejections: `checkCandidate` refuses the locative after *do* (`"meste" is s6 of mesto, not s2`), a non-form, a missing gap, a far trigger, an unknown word, digits, a verb gap after a preposition and *Na dni.* for the locative. In the stage, a writer that puts every noun in the locative gets all of them rejected by the rules, and phrases the review turns down are dropped. `c4.exercises` reports an answer in the wrong case and an option that is no real form.
  - Also: hash ids, kinds, phrases, options from other real forms, a second mock run that changes nothing, a dry run that writes nothing.

## Differences from the specification, and why
- **Prompts live in the stage folder** (`exercises/prompts.ts`), as docs/work-packages.md asks for stages that call the model, not in `model/prompts/`.
- **Triggers: 119 verbs**, more than "about 80", to cover the dative and instrumental lessons and the genitive verbs; 221 in all.
- **YAML fields beyond the contract** (they do not reach the content set): triggers `forms` (spellings when the morphology lacks them: *kde*, *koľko*); lessons `pronouns` (the dative lesson's *on*, *ona*); patterns `stem`, `stems`, `prefer`, `everyday`, `model`.
- **The adjective pattern *páví* is left out**: it is rare and the morphology gives no lemma with it among the common words. `otcov` is there (possessive adjectives).
- **`choice_meaning` options** are the English meanings of the trigger's contrast group (*na* + acc "onto, for" vs *na* + loc "on, at"), built by rule like the form options.
- **The mixed lesson** gets every 5th slot of every bucket, so its exercises are not repeats of other lessons.
- **The two-case rule** (a gap after *na*, *v*, *za* ... must not also be the other case) is stricter than the spec: an animate masculine accusative after *za* (= the genitive) is always rejected, so those slots fall to other triggers.
- **Outside the owned paths**: `tools/pipeline/src/stages/index.ts` registers the three stages at the `// C4` place (between `sentences` and `scenarios`); `tools/pipeline/src/stages/sentences/sentences.test.ts` leaves the C4 checks out of its mini validate, because the mini dataset's lessons have three steps, not five (one filter line). `validate.test.ts` already names its stages (`MINI_STAGES`) after the merge.

## Known gaps and follow ups
- **No live fill**: `content/generated/exercises.jsonl` does not exist yet, so a full `validate` without the mock bank has no exercises and the lesson steps are empty (`c4.lessons` fails). Use `exercises --fill mock` for development builds.
- The mock sentences are just trigger + gap; the real ones will be longer, and the rules and review will reject some. The 1.25 overshoot is meant for that; a bucket below 40 after the live run gets a warning, and a second `exercises --fill live --per-bucket 50` fills only the open slots.
- `packages/testkit/mini/lessons.jsonl` still has three-step lessons; a later update of the mini set to five steps would let `sentences.test.ts` keep every check.
- The vocabulary rule uses the rank of the best lemma of a spelling, so a rare word that shares a spelling with a common one passes; the review is the second line there.

# C3 Example sentences
Status: done with gaps

## What was built
The stage `sentences` in `tools/pipeline/src/stages/sentences/`, its three model routes, two validate checks, and a real-data sample for tests.

| Piece | Where | Does |
|---|---|---|
| Harvest | `harvest.ts` | Tatoeba: Slovak sentences linked to English (the English one with the lowest id; of identical Slovak texts the lowest id). UD Slovak SNK: the three `.conllu` splits, each word placed in the text by offsets (multiword tokens such as *Aby* = *aby* + *by* have no single analysis and are left out). A cheap first cut keeps 4 to 12 words and no digits |
| Lexicon | `lexicon.ts` | Lemmas with rank, top forms and preposition cases (`flags.cases`, or the set's `triggers.jsonl`); the readings (lemma, one tag) of the forms the harvest uses plus every form of the top 3,000. A capital inside a sentence matches only capitalised forms (names), a small letter only small ones; *Tom* at the start of a sentence is a name |
| Tokens | `analyze.ts` | `tokenize` from `@app/ling`, then every reading. Treebank words keep the readings that fit the treebank's lemma and SNK tag (mapped by C1's `mapSnk`; a typo `:q` or a comparative leaves none). Other words are narrowed by four rules: a lemma on the frequency list beats lemmas off it (*a* the conjunction, not the letter), a vocative needs a comma or "!" beside it, a preposition limits the case of the phrase after it, an adjective agrees with its noun |
| Rules | `rules.ts` | `sentenceProblem`: `length` (4–12 words), `number`, `punctuation` (quotes, brackets; capital start, final mark), `unknown`, `rare` (a word with no candidate lemma ranked ≤ 3,000). `targetsOf`: a word is a target when one lemma and one tag are left and (form, tag) is one of the lemma's `topForms`; otherwise `ambiguous` or `not-top-form`. `checkGenerated`: the same rules, then the requested form with the requested lemma and tag (`target-missing`, `not-top-form`). `difficulty` = 0.35 · length + 0.45 · mean log rank + 0.2 · rarest log rank, in 0–1. `choose`: up to 3 per lemma, easiest first, the easiest of each different form first; Tatoeba before treebank (+0.05, needs a translation) before generated (+0.1) |
| Prompts | `prompts.ts` | `content.sentences` v1 (medium, 40 items per request; the allowed vocabulary, the 3,000 lemmas, is in the system prompt, so it is identical and cacheable across requests; each item names lemma, form, tag and the tag in words, and up to two glosses), `content.review` v1 (medium, 40 per request: natural, formCorrect, corrected `en`, problem), `content.translate` v1 (low, 25 per request). Mocks: `<Form> <three allowed words>.`, review approves, translation `[mock] <sk>` |
| Stage | `index.ts` | Harvest → tokens → rules → choose real ones → translate the chosen treebank ones → choose again → generate for lemmas with fewer than 2 (items to reach 3, forms not yet shown first) → rules → review → final choice → `content/build/sentences/sentences.jsonl` (a `rows` part of `sentences.jsonl`), `report.md`, `report.json`. Model rows (accepted generated sentences and treebank translations) go to `content/generated/sentences.jsonl`; reruns reuse them and send nothing twice |
| Checks | `index.ts` | `sentences.rules`: each link matches its token's lemma and tag; unverified rows have a `sourceRef`, 4–12 words, every token lemma ranked ≤ 3,000 and linked forms among the top forms. `sentences.coverage`: lemmas ranked ≤ 3,000 with fewer than 2 sentences; an issue in a complete build when more than 2 % are short, else a warning |
| Sample | `fixtures/`, `sample.ts` | `make-sample.py` cuts real Tatoeba (202 sentences, 40 with one word outside the mini dataset) and treebank (35) sentences that the mini dataset can tokenize; `seedSentencesSample(paths)` puts them into `content/raw/` of a temporary root |

Row ids: `tat-<number>`; `ud-<split>-<orig_file_sentence>` such as `ud-train-001-1` (stable across the sample and the full file; the global `sent_id` contains `:` and `.`, which the id pattern refuses); `gen-<first 8 hex of sha256(textSk)>`. `sourceRef`: the Tatoeba number, the treebank's `sent_id`, or `content.sentences:<request hash prefix>`. Licences: Tatoeba `CC BY 2.0 FR`, treebank `CC BY-SA 4.0`, generated `CC BY 4.0`. Every row has `verified: false`. Tokens cover every token of the tokenizer (punctuation too, without a lemma); a word gets `lemmaId` when one lemma is left and `tag` when one tag is left; links exist only for the lemmas a sentence was chosen for, with `ord` their display order.

## How to run and test it
- `pnpm test --filter pipeline` or `pnpm check` (passes). The C3 tests take about 2 s.
- Full run: `pnpm run pipeline fetch --source tatoeba,ud-slovak-snk` (after C1/C2's `morph`, `freq`, `dict`) → `sentences` → `sentences --fill dry-run` → (approved) `sentences --fill live --max-spend <usd>` → `validate`.
- Done when, and the tests (`sentences.test.ts`):
  - Mini dataset in mock mode: `sentences --lexicon <mini> --fill mock` on the sample gives every one of the 31 nouns at least two sentences (and no lemma more than three), with Tatoeba, treebank (mock translated) and generated rows; the mini dataset with its `sentences.jsonl` replaced by the output passes `validate` with every check of every stage and the mini registry.
  - Every row carries `source`, `license` (checked per source) and `sourceRef`; ids match the contract pattern.
  - Rejections: `Idem do mesta.` (3 words) and a 13-word sentence → `length`; *kniha* with only the top 40 counted → `rare`; *bicykel* → `unknown`; digits → `number`; quotes → `punctuation`; a generated sentence without *mesta* → `target-missing`, with *mestami* (not a top form) → `not-top-form`. The checks fail a row without `sourceRef`, a link off its token, 2 words, a token lemma ranked 4,000, a link to a non-top form, and coverage short in a complete build.
  - Dry run: prints each route's request count and estimate and the total, sends nothing, writes nothing to `content/generated`; a second dry run after a mock fill has nothing left to send; a live fill above `--max-spend` is refused before anything is sent.
  - Also: rule narrowing (*v práci* → s6; *tá kniha* → s1), treebank readings (*to* → s1n from `PFns1`), `choose` prefers different forms.

## Differences from the specification, and why
- **The prompts live in `stages/sentences/prompts.ts`**, not `model/prompts/` or `packages/ai/prompts/`: the package owns only the stage folder. A later shared surface PR can move them.
- **`content.translate`** is a new pipeline route (the batch twin of `reader.translate`); the architecture's route table has no batch route for translating treebank sentences.
- **Rows marked `verified: true` are exempt from the length, rank and top-form rules and from `sourceRef`**: the mini dataset's hand-written rows (some `source: generated`, verified, without `sourceRef`, one of three words) were checked by a person. The stage itself writes only `verified: false`.
- **One C1 test changed** (`stages/validate/validate.test.ts`, one line plus a comment): validating the mini dataset as a complete build now also reports `sentences.coverage`, as the mini dataset has fewer than two sentences for most lemmas. The test already names the stages it runs explicitly. `stages/index.ts` registers `sentences` after `dict`.
- **Links only for chosen lemmas.** The mini dataset links every token (ord 0). The stage links a sentence to the lemmas it was chosen for, so `ord` is the display order of at most three sentences per lemma.
- **Generation asks for enough items to reach three sentences**, not two, for a lemma short of two, leaving room for rejections.
- **Licence of generated sentences**: `CC BY 4.0`, the licence the mini dataset uses for hand-written content. Please confirm.
- A token that two homograph lemmas chose in the same sentence goes to the better ranked lemma.

## Known gaps and follow ups
- **No request has reached the API**, so `content/generated/sentences.jsonl` does not exist yet and validate on the full build fails `sentences.coverage` (1,941 + 355 of 3,000 short) until the paid fill; use `--fill mock` for development builds.
- Real sentences cover only about a quarter of the top 3,000; three quarters of the target comes from generation. More rules would keep more Tatoeba sentences: 12,114 target words stay ambiguous, mostly nominative/accusative (*mesto*) and verb past forms that differ only by person. Function words with several ranked lemmas (*ale*, *aj*, *tak*, *po*) are never resolved by rule and always go to the model.
- The rules accept a non-target word when any of its readings is a common lemma.
- The review only judges; a rejected item is not asked again with a new request (its cached answer would repeat). A rerun with `promptVersion` raised, or a second round with an attempt number in the item, would retry them.
- The fingerprint covers the stage folder and `lib/`, not `stages/morph/snk.ts` (`mapSnk`): after changing the tag mapping, run `sentences --force`.
- Difficulty weights are a first guess; nothing yet calibrates them against learners.

## Notes for packages that depend on this one
- **I2**: `pnpm run pipeline sentences --fill dry-run` (expect about $15), then first a trial: `sentences --fill live --sample 25 --max-spend 2`, read `content/generated/sentences.jsonl` and `content/build/sentences/report.md`, then `sentences --fill live --max-spend 16`. `--max-spend` bounds the whole run (all three routes together), and before generation a live run checks that generation plus the estimated review fit. Commit `content/generated/sentences.jsonl` afterwards. If a run stops at the review limit, the generated answers are cached in `content/build/model-cache/`, so the next run pays only for the review.
- `--sample N` limits the lemmas to fill; `--top N` changes the 3,000; `--generated <path>` and `--lexicon <set dir>` are for tests and local runs.
- B-packages reading `ling.lemma_sentence`: `ord` 0–2 per lemma, `tag` is the form's tag in that sentence, `token_index` indexes `tokens`, which include punctuation tokens.

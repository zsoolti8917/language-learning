# F9 Mini Slovak dataset
Status: done

## What was built
A complete content set in `packages/testkit/mini/`, and the module `@app/testkit/mini` that reads it.

| File | Rows | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| `lemmas.jsonl` | 72 | The ids of the table, keys like `mesto|noun|n|1`, pattern, aspect, rank, top forms, flags |
| `forms.jsonl` | 601 | Every form of every lemma, from the SNK morphology database, mapped to contract tags |
| `senses.jsonl` | 87 | English glosses, `source: manual`, `verified: true` |
| `sentences.jsonl` | 40 | 18 real Tatoeba sentences (`tat-<id>`, CC BY 2.0 FR), 22 written for the set (`gen-<hash>`). Every token is tagged. Together they link all 72 lemmas |
| `triggers.jsonl` | 22 | 14 rows for the 12 prepositions (`v` and `na` have an `acc` and a `loc` row with a contrast group), `verb-pomahat`, `verb-vidiet`, `num-pat`, 5 question words (`q-koho-acc`, `q-co-acc`, `q-komu`, `q-kde`, `q-s-kym`) |
| `patterns.jsonl` | 16 | The 14 noun patterns and `pekny`, `cudzi`, endings keyed by form tag |
| `lessons.jsonl` | 9 | The whole course. `acc-sg` and `loc-sg` have all five steps. The others have explain, triggers and checkpoint |
| `exercises.jsonl` | 89 | 33 for `acc-sg`, 33 for `loc-sg` (every bucket in every exercise step), 3 or 4 typed gaps for each other lesson |
| `scenarios.jsonl` | 2 | `scn-city-walk` (scenario), `scn-coach-loc` (case coach) |
| `library.jsonl` | 1 | `lib-001` "Môj deň", 77 words, A2, with a full `TextAnalysis` and `lemmaCounts` |
| `manifest.json` | | Version `2026-11-17.1`, sources with sha256 checksums and licences, row counts |

`mini/index.ts` exports:
- `MINI_DIR`: the folder, for `loadContent(db, MINI_DIR)`.
- `MINI_LEMMA_IDS`: lemma → id for the 72 lemmas.
- `loadMini()`: the parsed set (`ContentSet`), cached, with the maps `lemmaById`, `formsByLemma`, `triggerById`, `patternById`, `lessonById`, `exerciseById`, `sentenceById`.
- `miniFormMap(lemmaId)`: form → tags, in the shape of `ExerciseItem.formMap`.
- `readContentFile(dir, file)` and `readContentSet(dir)`: read and parse any content folder with the `CONTENT_FILES` schemas. A parse error names the file and line.

## How to run and test it
`pnpm test --filter testkit`, or `pnpm check`. `mini/dataset.test.ts` (42 tests) proves that:
- every row of the 10 files parses with its schema, the manifest parses, and its counts match;
- the 72 ids and lemmas equal the table;
- every link resolves: form, sense and trigger lemmas, lemma patterns, pattern model words, sentence tokens and links, lesson steps (triggers, exercises in a step of their own lesson), exercise trigger, pattern and lesson, scenario triggers, library analysis lemmas, triggers and `lemmaCounts`;
- every exercise answer is a form of its target lemma with the tag the exercise claims (`s4` for a noun, `s4f` for an agreeing word), the gap holds the first answer, the gender and pattern are those of the noun, and wrong choice options are real forms of other cases;
- every pattern's endings rebuild the forms of its model word;
- every noun has all its slots, every lemma has forms, a gloss, a rank and top forms;
- the exercises, trigger, pattern, scenario and library ids that the F2 examples use exist, with the same sentence, answers and features, and every form and tag of their `formMap` is in the set.

## Differences from the specification, and why
- **Lemma 28 `ľudia`** is a lemma of its own (plural only, `flags.pluralOnly`), as the table says. SNK files these forms under `človek`, so lemma 27 `človek` holds only its singular forms.
- **`ten`** holds the forms SNK files under `ten`, `tá` and `to` (pronoun readings), so it has all genders. **`on`** and **`ona`** are the SNK lemmas as they are (`on` covers masculine forms and the masculine plural `oni`/`ony`, `ona` the feminine ones). `ono` is not in the set.
- **Tags.** Nouns get number and case; adjectives, `môj`, `ten`, `on`, `ona` and the numerals get number, case and gender; `ja`, `ty`, `kto` and `čo` get `x` and the case; verbs get `v:` plus the SNK tag without its leading `V` (`v:Kesa+`); everything else gets an empty tag. Left out: comparative and superlative forms of adjectives and adverbs (their tags could not say so), negated verb forms (SNK files them under `nebyť`, `nemať`, …), substantive readings (`môj` meaning "my husband") and forms SNK marks as errors.
- **Ranks and top forms** come from the OpenSubtitles list (FrequencyWords `sk_full.txt`): form counts are summed per lemma and the 72 lemmas are ranked 1 to 72 among themselves. They are not global ranks. Homographs count for every lemma they belong to.
- **Lesson steps** are numbered from 0, as in the F2 example. The seven lessons besides `acc-sg` and `loc-sg` have three steps (explain, triggers, checkpoint) so the course is complete; the specification asks only for their exercises.
- **The F2 sentence `tat-482913`** does not exist in Tatoeba. "Idem do mesta." is Tatoeba sentence 8214994, so the set has `tat-8214994`. The five F2 exercise ids are kept as they are, although they are not hashes; every other exercise id is `ex-` + the first 10 hex digits of sha1(`kind|sentence|start:end`), and authored sentence ids are `gen-` + 8 hex digits of sha1(text).
- **Trigger ids** for numerals and question words had no convention: `num-pat`, `q-<word>[-case]`.
- Licences: Tatoeba rows carry `CC BY 2.0 FR`; text written for this set carries `CC BY 4.0`. Change it if the project picks another licence.
- The branch also carries `main` (F1, F2, F7), because the worktree started from F1 only.

## Known gaps and follow ups
- No form is unverified. Every inflected form in the forms file comes from SNK, and every word of every Slovak sentence, exercise, option, trigger example, lesson title, scenario and the library text was checked against SNK (as a form of any lemma). Words outside the 72 lemmas (e.g. `hovoríme`, `veľa`, `detí`) carry no lemma id in tokens.
- The generator was a one-off script outside the repository (it reads the SNK file, which is not in git). The JSONL files are now the source; edit them by hand and the tests check them. C1 replaces forms, ranks and top forms with real data for the same ids.
- SNK gaps show through: no `ňho`/`doň` forms for `on`, and only the SNK vocatives.

## Notes for packages that depend on this one
- Load into a database: `loadContent(db, MINI_DIR)` (F3). Read from memory: `loadMini()`; treat the result as read only, it is shared.
- Exercise rows hold no `formMap` or `siblingEndings`; build them with `miniFormMap(lemmaId)` and the pattern endings (`patternById`).
- Two exercises have phrase answers (`novú prácu`, `novom dome`): `target.lemmaIds` lists one lemma per word, in order. Three accept two answers (`človeku`/`človekovi`, `mu`/`jemu`, and `dievčatá`/`dievčence`).
- The `dat-pron` pronoun exercises target `on` (39) and `ona` (40) with genders `m_anim` and `f`.
- Placement: every one of the nine lessons has at least two `cloze` exercises.
